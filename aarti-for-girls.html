Why we chose this partnership

A shared belief in her potential

Anaya exists to help girls and women find their voice and shape their own lives. Aarti brings education and care together, creating the conditions in which a girl can learn, belong, and begin to imagine a future she chooses.

Mission alignment

A focus on girls’ education, care, and the opportunity to shape their own futures.

Care that meets real needs

Education, meals, and housing where needed, responding to different circumstances.

An ongoing relationship

Individual progress reports and conversations with the team help us understand needs and where Anaya can help.

Our three pillars in practice

How this partnership carries our mission forward

01
Foundation

Safety and Basic Needs

Anaya’s contribution helps fund meals and housing where needed. Aarti provides the daily care and stability that support the girls’ lives and learning.

02
Education

Learning and Belonging

Our support helps girls access education. Aarti’s teachers provide individual guidance, reinforce foundational skills, and help girls participate in classroom life.

03
Self-actualization

Confidence and Well-being

Aarti reports that some girls now interact more comfortably with teachers and classmates. Teachers encourage them to express ideas, ask questions, and participate in class, building confidence to speak up.
